## Contexte

Le MDU dispose de designs et d’atomes causals dispersés :
- `designs/kg-causal.yaml`
- `designs/kg-causal-engine.yaml`
- `designs/causal-semantic-bridge.yaml`
- `designs/narrative-causal-fusion.yaml`
